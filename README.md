## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
The agricultural supply chain involves multiple intermediaries between farmers and consumers. This results in reduced profits for farmers, increased prices for consumers, and delays in product delivery.

The challenge is to develop a digital solution that directly connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, and bulk buyers while improving agricultural distribution and logistics.



## Proposed Solution
Develop an AI-powered agricultural digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

The platform will:

Allow farmers to list and sell their agricultural products.

Enable consumers and retailers to purchase directly from farmers.

Provide transparent pricing and real-time market information.

Connect farmers with available transportation and logistics providers.

Use AI to predict demand and suggest suitable selling prices.

Reduce unnecessary intermediaries in the agricultural supply chain.

Innovation and Uniqueness:

A unified platform combining direct agricultural trading, AI-based demand forecasting, logistics management, and transparent transactions.

## Technical Approach
Technologies to be Used:

Frontend: React.js

Mobile Application: Flutter

Backend: Python (FastAPI)

Database: PostgreSQL

AI/ML: Python, Scikit-learn

Maps and Navigation: Google Maps API

Cloud: AWS / Firebase

Implementation Methodology:

Farmer and buyer registration.

Agricultural product listing and discovery.

AI-based demand and price prediction.

Order placement and payment management.

Logistics and route optimization.

Order tracking and delivery confirmation.

Workflow:

Farmer → Product Listing → Digital Marketplace → Buyer Order → Logistics Assignment → Delivery → Payment



## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility:

Can be developed using existing web and mobile technologies.

Farmers can access the platform through smartphones.

Cloud infrastructure supports scalability.

Existing digital payment systems can be integrated.

Potential Challenges:

Limited digital literacy among farmers.

Transportation and storage difficulties.

Fluctuating agricultural prices.

Product quality and trust management.

Internet connectivity in rural areas.

Strategies to Overcome Challenges:

Provide a simple, multilingual interface.

Partner with local logistics providers and FPOs.

Introduce product verification and ratings.

Support low-bandwidth access.

Provide transparent pricing and transaction records.



## Impact and Benefits
Economic Benefits:

Increased earnings for farmers.

Reduced purchasing costs for consumers.

Better market access for small-scale farmers.

Reduced dependency on intermediaries.

Social Benefits:

Improved digital inclusion in rural communities.

Better connections between farmers and buyers.

Increased opportunities for FPOs.

Environmental Benefits:

Optimized delivery routes.

Reduced transportation inefficiencies.

Better demand forecasting to minimize food wastage.
